# Healings & Derek — Wedding Story

A romantic, editorial wedding story concept inspired by WithJoy’s “Our Story” pages.

## Preview

Double-click `preview.ps1` in PowerShell to open the story in your default browser, or open `index.html` directly. Open `story-notes.html` for a printable prompt sheet and copyable reply template.

The photos in the main page are illustrative stock images. Replace them with Healings and Derek’s own photos when available. Story prompts are intentionally generic until the couple’s details are provided.

## Personalize

Share any of these details to tailor the story: how you met, favorite memories or milestones, wedding date and location, preferred colors or mood, and photos with captions if desired. Rough notes are enough. The story-notes page autosaves in the current browser; use its Copy button and paste the notes into your chat reply. Attach couple photos to the same reply for the gallery.
